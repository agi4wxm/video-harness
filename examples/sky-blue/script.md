# Why is the sky blue?
> 示例旁白：短句，段落之间留停顿。

Look up on a clear day, and the sky is blue. But sunlight is white.

So where does the blue come from?

[pause 0.3]
Zoom into the air, and you find tiny molecules of nitrogen and oxygen.

When sunlight hits them, it bounces off in every direction. Blue light bounces about five times more than red.

So wherever you look, scattered blue light is coming at you.

At sunset, light crosses much more air. The blue is scattered away before it reaches you, and only the reds are left.
